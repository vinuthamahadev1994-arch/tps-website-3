1 Scope & Corporate Commitment

Total Procurement Solutions (hereinafter referred to as "TPS", "we", "us", or "our"), established in Bangalore, Karnataka on 31 May 2010 under the leadership of Mr. Vivek P. Hegde, operates as a premier temperature-controlled road freight and cold-chain logistics service provider.

This Privacy Policy outlines our strict protocols regarding the collection, processing, transmission, and retention of business information, consignment details, GPS telemetry, and thermal sensor logs obtained through our website, freight booking forms, email communications, and on-board IoT vehicle tracking units.

Our Core Privacy Promise

TPS does not sell, lease, monetize, or trade any customer data, commercial trade secrets, supplier lists, or consignment schedules to third parties under any circumstances.

2 Information We Collect

To fulfill cold-chain road transportation agreements with pharmaceutical manufacturers, food processors, dairy suppliers, and FMCG distributors, we collect only necessary operational data:

  • Consignor & Consignee Profiles: Registered company name, corporate address, GSTIN, point-of-contact name, corporate email address, and active telephone / mobile numbers.
  • Shipment & Cargo Details: Nature of perishable cargo (meat, poultry, ice cream, vaccines, frozen vegetables, confectioneries), net tonnage, target storage temperature band (-25°C to +25°C), packaging specs, and invoice value.
  • Routing & Schedule Coordinates: Exact pickup warehouse/plant locations, drop-off distribution depots, planned dispatch schedules, docking bay constraints, and gate entry contact personnel.
  • Digital Form Submissions: Enquiries submitted via tpsindustries.com or direct WhatsApp / email freight estimation requests.

3 IoT Sensors & Temperature Telematics

All reefer trucks in our fleet (including Mahindra Bolero Maxi, Tata Ultra, and Eicher Pro refrigerated units) are equipped with digital data loggers and multi-point temperature probes:

Continuous Thermal Logs

Sensors record interior box temperatures at calibrated intervals (every 5-15 minutes) to ensure cold-chain integrity from warehouse dispatch to delivery.

Real-Time GPS Telematics

Vehicle coordinates, route waypoints, transit speeds, and halt durations are logged for live consignment tracking and ETA predictions.

These telematics records are archived for verification and shared with consignors as digital temperature audit reports upon trip completion or inspection requests.

4 How We Utilize Your Information

Information gathered by TPS is utilized strictly for executing logistics services and compliance, including:

  • Planning optimal refrigerated freight routes to minimize transit time and protect thermal stability.
  • Generating e-Way bills, consignment notes (LR/Lorry Receipts), and statutory transit documentation required under Indian Motor Vehicle regulations.
  • Delivering automated WhatsApp, SMS, and email notifications to client dispatch managers regarding vehicle arrival, loading, en-route milestones, and offloading.
  • Issuing transparent GST invoices, payment reconciliation, and customer relationship management.

5 Commercial Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality

TPS recognizes that cargo manifests, proprietary cold-store recipes, institutional drug distribution lists, and contractual freight rates represent valuable commercial intellectual property. We implement strict internal confidentiality agreements across all 20+ operations staff, drivers, and fleet supervisors.

Non-Disclosure Guarantee

No information regarding client consignment volumes, receiving vendor names, or negotiated tariff schedules is ever disclosed to competitors, marketing brokers, or unauthorized entities.

6 Regulatory & Statutory Disclosures

We only share consignment and billing information under mandated statutory circumstances:

  • Statutory Transport Authorities: Presentation of e-Way bills, Lorry Receipts, and tax invoices to Regional Transport Offices (RTO), GST Flying Squads, and State Border Checkposts in accordance with Indian transport laws.
  • Insurance Underwriters: In the rare event of transit accident or temperature excursion claims, data loggers and consignment values are provided to licensed surveyors for claim settlement.
  • Court Orders / Judicial Process: Under binding orders issued by competent judicial courts in Bangalore, Karnataka.

7 Storage, Security & Retention

TPS maintains industry-standard security safeguards to protect digital records and telemetry streams from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or loss:

Encrypted Storage

Consignment documents and digital invoices are secured in encrypted databases with role-based access.

7-Year Retention

Statutory GST records and freight audit logs are maintained for 7 years as mandated by tax authorities.

8 Client Data Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, consignor and consignee authorized representatives possess the following rights regarding their corporate data:

  • Right to Access: Request certified copies of historical temperature telematics and delivery receipts.
  • Right to Correction: Promptly update contact details, GSTIN, or billing addresses in our master carrier directory.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of non-statutory enquiry records once contract obligations are fulfilled.
