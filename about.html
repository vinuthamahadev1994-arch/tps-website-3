Setting the Benchmark in Cold-Chain Road Transport

Headquartered in Bangalore, Karnataka, Total Procurement Solutions is dedicated to preserving temperature-sensitive products from origin to shelf.

Total Procurement Solutions was established on 31 May 2010 under the leadership of Mr. Vivek P. Hegde, a B.Sc. Computer Graduate with distinguished experience in the Merchant Navy. Bringing the stringent operational discipline and navigation precision of international maritime logistics to domestic freight, Mr. Hegde has positioned TPS as one of Bangalore's most trusted cold-chain providers.

We specialize in the safe and temperature-controlled carriage of meat, poultry, dairy, frozen foods, ice creams, confectionery, and critical medical supplies. Every truck in our fleet is fitted with advanced insulation and specialized reefer units capable of maintaining constant temperatures between -25°C and +25°C regardless of external ambient heat.

Why Cold-Chain Integrity Matters

A single thermal spike can render an entire consignment of life-saving vaccines or frozen meats unfit for human consumption. At Total Procurement Solutions, we implement preventive controls:

Dual-Sensor Temperature Probes

Continuously record intake and discharge air temperatures, storing logs for audit compliance.

Strip Curtains & Thermal Airtight Seals

Heavy-duty food-grade PVC curtains prevent cold loss during cargo loading and unloading stops.

Sanitized Fleet Interiors

Every vehicle undergoes deep cleaning and food-safe sanitization between transit cycles.
