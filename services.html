Meat & Poultry Transportation Services

Total Procurement Solutions is one of Bangalore's most reputed providers of fresh, chilled, and frozen meat transit. We recognize that meat is susceptible to microbiological contamination if temperatures rise even momentarily.

  • Continuous Deep Chill: Maintained at precise sub-zero temperatures using multi-stage thermoking cooling.
  • Hygienic Interior Linings: Food-grade stainless steel / seamless GRP walls sanitized after every haul.
  • Fast Cross-Docking: Expedited unloading preventing exposure to warm ambient air.

Medicine & Vaccine Transportation Services

Medicines, biologics, diagnostic kits, and insulin require uninterrupted climate control. TPS operates dedicated pharmaceutical logistics vans with dual digital data loggers and automatic alerts.

  • GDP Validation: Calibrated probes measuring temperature every 60 seconds with printable audit reports.
  • Emergency Backup Cooling: Secondary standby generators protect against engine downtime.
  • Security & Integrity: Tamper-evident door seals and real-time door-opening sensor alarms.

Ice Cream & Dairy Dessert Transportation

Ice creams require an extreme -25°C environment to prevent fat separation, ice crystal formation, or textural damage. Our insulated containers ensure delivery with zero melting.

  • Eutectic & Blower Systems: High-efficiency cold retention even during multi-drop city delivery.
  • Thermal Curtains: Prevents heat influx when driver opens rear doors for retailer supply.
  • Supermarket & Parlour Deliveries: Prompt scheduling for retail chains across Bangalore.

Frozen Food & Vegetable Logistics

From green peas, sweet corn, and French fries to ready-to-cook snacks, our trucks keep frozen food consignments solidly frozen from food processing hubs to cold storages and distributors.

  • Even Air Circulation: Heavy ribbed flooring allowing pressurized sub-zero airflow beneath pallets.
  • Zero Moisture Condensation: Active humidity balancing preserving outer cartons and labels.
  • Full Truck Load (FTL) & Part Loads: Flexible options for processors and distributors.

Cakes, Pastries & Chocolate Transportation

Chocolates and artisan pastries demand gentle driving, pneumatic suspension, and exact temperature balancing to avoid melting, blooming, or structural deformation.

  • Shock-Absorbing Transit: Minimizes road vibrations to protect intricate fondant and tier cakes.
  • Constant +16°C for Chocolates: Prevents cocoa butter migration and keeps packaging dry.
  • Bakery Chain Distribution: Timed morning deliveries to retail pastry shops.
