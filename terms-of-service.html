1 Carrier Status & Agreement Acceptance

These Terms of Service constitute a legally binding agreement between Total Procurement Solutions (hereinafter "TPS"), founded in 2010 by Mr. Vivek P. Hegde in Bangalore, Karnataka, and any consignor, shipper, booking broker, or consignee engaging our refrigerated road transportation services.

TPS acts as a specialized common carrier governed by the provisions of the Carriage by Road Act, 2007 and the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 of India. Tendering cargo for carriage, issuing a Lorry Receipt (LR), or booking a reefer vehicle through our digital portals confirms complete acceptance of these terms.

2 Scope of Services & Temperature Regimes

TPS provides temperature-controlled road freight throughout Bangalore, Karnataka, South India, and across national corridors for temperature-sensitive cargo. Our fleet maintains the following operating categories:

Deep Freeze (-25°C to -18°C)

For commercial ice creams, frozen poultry, dressed meat, seafood, and frozen green peas / vegetables.

Chilled & Pharma (+2°C to +8°C)

For clinical trial samples, biologicals, life-saving vaccines, fresh dairy products, and specialized serums.

Controlled Ambient (+15°C to +25°C)

For premium chocolates, confectioneries, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and dry syrups.

Dedicated Reefer Vans

From 1.5-ton Bolero reefers to 20-ton multi-axle refrigerated container trucks with standby electric power.

3 Shipper Obligations & Cargo Handover

The consignor acknowledges that refrigerated transport units are designed to maintain cargo temperature during transit, not to freeze or chill uncooled products. The shipper undertakes the following mandatory responsibilities:

  • Pre-Cooling Cargo: All perishable consignments must be thoroughly pre-cooled to the specified transport temperature prior to vehicle loading.
  • Standard Cold-Chain Packaging: Goods must be packaged in robust, moisture-resistant, insulated corrugated boxes or food-grade crates that withstand stack pressure and allow optimal airflow.
  • Statutory Transit Documentation: The shipper is solely responsible for generating valid e-Way bills, tax invoices, consignment declarations, and FSSAI / Drug Controller licenses required by law.
Uncooled Cargo Disclaimer

TPS reserves the absolute right to refuse cargo tendered at temperatures significantly warmer than the booked set-point, or mark the Lorry Receipt (LR) with an uncooled cargo disclaimer.

4 Vehicle Placement & Loading Protocols

TPS will place the agreed class of reefer vehicle at the designated pickup bay on the confirmed schedule. To preserve the cold chain:

  • Vehicles will undergo 30 to 45 minutes of pre-cooling prior to door opening at the loading bay.
  • Loading should be conducted through an insulated dock seal or protected strip curtains to prevent ambient air ingress.
  • Cargo must never block the evaporator discharge plenum or return air bulkheads, maintaining free airflow along vehicle floor T-channels.

6 Temperature Monitoring & Joint Delivery Verification

Every trip is tracked with electronic sensors. At destination:

  • The consignee receiving agent and TPS pilot will jointly observe and record cargo surface and air return temperature before unloading.
  • Proof of Delivery (POD) must be endorsed with date, arrival time, temperature, and signature.
  • Any claim regarding temperature excursion must be endorsed in writing on the Lorry Receipt immediately and notified to TPS within 24 hours of delivery.

7 Carrier Liability & Transit Insurance

Carrier liability is governed strictly by the Carriage by Road Act, 2007. Key liability conditions include:

Mandatory Transit Cargo Insurance

Consignors are strongly advised to obtain comprehensive all-risk Marine / Transit Cargo Insurance covering full invoice value against accidental physical loss, thermal damage, and transit hazards. TPS carrier liability for undeclared value cargo is limited as provided under statutory carriage laws.

TPS is not liable for cargo damage resulting from inherent vice, improper packaging, hidden biological decay, incorrect pre-loading temperatures, or consignor-directed delays.

8 Force Majeure & En-Route Contingencies

TPS shall not be deemed in breach of contract for transit delays, schedule revisions, or delivery postponements caused by events beyond reasonable operational control, including but not limited to:

  • Acts of God, flash floods, cyclones, severe monsoon highway breaches, landslides, or earthquakes.
  • National / State highway bandhs, civil disturbances, political blockades, unannounced border strikes, or curfew restrictions.
  • Arbitrary vehicle seizure or protracted inspection by statutory highway surveillance flying squads.

9 Tariffs, Payment Terms & Carrier's Lien

Freight charges are billed according to confirmed quotations or contractual rate schedules:

  • Payment Deadlines: For corporate account holders, invoices are payable within agreed credit windows (typically 15 to 30 days). For spot bookings, freight is payable 50% advance upon loading and balance before unloading.
  • Diesel Escalation: Long-term contracts incorporate a transparent diesel fuel price variation formula pegged to prevailing state retail rates.
  • Carrier's Right of Lien: Under the Indian Contract Act and Carriage by Road Act, TPS reserves a general and particular lien on any cargo in transit or storage for unpaid freight or demurrage dues.

Exclusive Jurisdiction

The competent courts situated in Bangalore, Karnataka, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any legal claims, proceedings, or arbitration arising under this agreement.
