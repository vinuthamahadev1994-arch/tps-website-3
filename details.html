Cold-Chain Engineering & Quality Assurance Details

Preventive thermal controls and standard operating procedures executed on every Total Procurement Solutions dispatch.

1. Pre-Cooling & Dual-Sensor Calibration

Containers are pre-cooled to the required cargo set-point prior to dock loading. Dual return-air and supply-air probes log temperature every 60 seconds with printable trip audit reports.

2. 100mm PUF Insulation & Airtight Multi-Lip Seals

High-density polyurethane foam (PUF) composite panels paired with heavy-duty EPDM rubber door gaskets and food-grade PVC strip curtains prevent ambient heat ingress.

3. FSSAI & GDP Sanitization Protocols

Every reefer interior undergoes pressure washing, food-safe anti-microbial sanitization, and deodorization between trips to prevent cross-contamination.

4. Electric Standby & Emergency Backup Support

Equipped with auxiliary standby power plugs and 24/7 highway mechanic response teams so cooling continues uninterrupted even during staging or halts.
